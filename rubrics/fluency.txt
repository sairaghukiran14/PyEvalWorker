# Rubric: Fluency (Version 1.0)
# Criterion: Is the text grammatically correct, natural, coherent, and free of disfluencies or artifacts?

Objective:
Evaluate language generation quality regardless of factual accuracy.

Scoring Scale (1 to 5):
- 5 (Flawless): Native-level fluency, perfect grammar, natural sentence flow, and flawless vocabulary.
- 4 (Good Fluency): Minor minor punctuation or awkward phrasing that does not impede comprehension.
- 3 (Understandable with Errors): Noticeable grammatical flaws, repetitions, or awkward constructions, but the general message is clear.
- 2 (Poor Fluency): Choppy, broken syntax, frequent errors, or repetitive loops making reading difficult.
- 1 (Incoherent): Severe word salad, corrupted tokens, or disjointed fragments.

Evaluation Procedure:
1. Examine syntax, grammar, spelling, and cohesion.
2. Assign a score from 1 to 5 with detailed feedback.
