# Rubric: Faithfulness / Groundedness (Version 1.0)
# Criterion: Is every factual claim in the answer directly supported by the retrieved context chunks?

Objective:
Evaluate whether the candidate answer contains any hallucinations, ungrounded inferences, or claims unsupported by the provided context chunks.

Scoring Scale (1 to 5):
- 5 (Completely Faithful): Every claim in the answer is directly supported by the context chunks. Zero hallucinations, embellishments, or outside knowledge introduced.
- 4 (Mostly Faithful): The answer is substantially supported. At most 1 minor trivial inference that is natural and does not alter factual correctness.
- 3 (Partially Faithful): The core point is supported, but contains 1-2 claims not mentioned in the context, or mild over-generalization.
- 2 (Mostly Unfaithful): Multiple key claims are not supported by the context or contradict the context chunks.
- 1 (Completely Hallucinated / Contradictory): The answer contradicts the context or invents substantial facts not present in any provided chunk.

Evaluation Procedure:
1. Extract all atomic factual claims from the candidate answer.
2. For each claim, check if it can be directly verified from the provided chunks.
3. Compute the ratio of supported claims to total claims.
4. Provide a structured explanation listing supported claims, unsupported claims, and reasoning.
