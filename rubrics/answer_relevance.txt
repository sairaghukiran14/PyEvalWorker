# Rubric: Answer Relevance (Version 1.0)
# Criterion: Does the answer directly address the user's question without evasiveness or irrelevant filler?

Objective:
Determine how directly, completely, and appropriately the answer addresses the specific question asked by the user.

Scoring Scale (1 to 5):
- 5 (Direct & Comprehensive): Directly answers the user's question completely, staying strictly on topic without irrelevant digressions or evasiveness.
- 4 (Relevant with Minor Digression): Directly answers the question, but includes minor tangential details or slightly unnecessary verbosity.
- 3 (Partially Relevant): Answers part of the question, or provides an overly broad response that leaves key parts of the user question unaddressed.
- 2 (Mostly Irrelevant / Evasive): Misses the core intent of the question, focuses on incidental details, or offers vague boilerplate without answering.
- 1 (Completely Irrelevant / Refusal to answer on-topic): Completely fails to address the question, answers a different question, or provides gibberish.

Evaluation Procedure:
1. Identify the core question and intent of the user.
2. Check if the answer provides the information requested.
3. Penalize evasive or off-topic information.
4. Assign a score from 1 to 5 and summarize reasoning.
